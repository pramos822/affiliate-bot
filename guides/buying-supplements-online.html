Buying dietary supplements online: a checklist before you order

Dietary supplements are sold everywhere online, often with big promises. This checklist helps you compare offers calmly. It is general information, not medical advice.

Talk to a health professional first

Supplements can interact with medication and are not right for everyone, especially during pregnancy or with chronic conditions. A doctor or pharmacist can tell you if a product makes sense for you.

Read the label, not the headline

Look for the full ingredient list and the amount per serving. In the US, supplement claims are not evaluated by the FDA, and supplements are not meant to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

Check the guarantee and the order type

Many online offers come with money-back guarantees of 60 to 180 days. Note how returns work, and check whether the order is a one-time purchase or an automatic subscription before paying.

Compare the price per month

Multi-bottle packages usually cost less per bottle, but more upfront. Also check shipping costs, which are sometimes charged only on single bottles.
