Starting affiliate marketing: tools and guides for beginners

Affiliate marketing means recommending products and earning a commission when someone buys through your link. It is simple to start, but results take time, and there is no guaranteed income.

Pick a topic you can talk about

Choose a niche you know or want to learn, where people actually buy products. It is easier to create useful content consistently about something you understand.

Always disclose your links

In the United States, the FTC requires a clear disclosure when you can earn from a link. Social networks have their own rules too. A short note such as "affiliate link, I may earn a commission" is the minimum.

Create content that helps

Platforms and search engines push back on pages that are only links. Reviews, comparisons, tutorials and honest pros and cons work better and last longer.
