Privacy Policy

Daily Deals Finder is a deals website and social media channel that shares products from affiliate programs (AliExpress, ClickBank, Digistore24). This policy explains what data we handle.

Information we collect

This website does not require an account, does not use contact forms and does not set its own cookies. We do not collect, sell or share personal information about visitors.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Hostinger. Our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its own privacy policy.

Affiliate links

When you click a product link, you are taken to the store's website. The store and affiliate network may use cookies to record that the purchase came from us, so we can earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Social media and automation

We use an automated tool to publish deals to our own accounts on Bluesky, Tumblr and Pinterest through their official APIs. The tool only accesses our own accounts (for example, to list our own Pinterest boards and create Pins on them). It does not access, store or share data from other users.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the project evolves. Questions can be sent through our social media profiles.
