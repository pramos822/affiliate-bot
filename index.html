Questions

How do you make money?

Some links are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Where do I pay, and who ships my order?

You pay on the seller's site. The seller handles payment, shipping, returns and support; we never process orders.

Are the prices up to date?

Prices are recorded when a deal is added and can change at any time. The seller's site always shows the current price.
