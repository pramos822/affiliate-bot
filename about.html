About Daily Deals Finder

Daily Deals Finder is a small independent deals website. We collect offers from affiliate programs (Digistore24, ClickBank and AliExpress) and show them in one place, with the price, the main details and a direct link to the seller.

How we choose deals

An automated tool helps us gather offers and store data such as prices, ratings, recent orders and discounts. We describe each product with the information the seller shows, without inventing reviews, ratings or results.

What we are not

We are not a store. We do not sell, ship or process payments. When you click a deal you go to the seller's website, which handles the order, delivery, refunds and support.

How we make money

Some links are affiliate links: if you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See our affiliate disclosure.

Health products

Supplement descriptions are for information only and have not been evaluated by the FDA. Talk to a health professional before using any supplement.
